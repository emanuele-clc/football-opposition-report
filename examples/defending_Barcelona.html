Descriptive. PPDA as commonly defined (opponent passes in their own 60% / tackles, interceptions and fouls in the same area); StatsBomb counts all duels, so values are not comparable with other providers. Pressures and the counterpress flag are StatsBomb definitions. PPDA also depends on how much the opponent has the ball: against a team that dominates possession opponents make few passes, so its PPDA can be high even when it presses high (read it with the pressures in the final third and the height). Seasons and StatsBomb data versions differ between teams (2015/16 vs 2023/24), so compare ranks loosely. Event data do not show positions off the ball, so the defensive line itself is not observed: the height of defensive actions is a proxy.
