Descriptive. PPDA as commonly defined (opponent passes in their own 60% / tackles, interceptions and fouls in the same area); Wyscout public data (Pappalardo et al. 2019, CC BY 4.0) defines duels and other defensive actions in its own way, so values are not comparable with other providers. Pressures are not in this data, so that part of the sheet is empty. PPDA also depends on how much the opponent has the ball: against a team that dominates possession opponents make few passes, so its PPDA can be high even when it presses high (read it with the height). Event data do not show positions off the ball, so the defensive line itself is not observed: the height of defensive actions is a proxy.
