Descriptive. xT values only where the ball goes, not how: it does not see pressure, defenders' positions or the receiver's situation, and failed passes are not penalised in the main figure (see 'threat given up'). The grid is fitted on the matches of the 10 thesis teams and their opponents (362 matches, 8833 open-play shots), seasons 2015/16 and 2023/24, so values in zones with few shots are rough. xT is not computed by sequence family on purpose: the families did not explain shots better than a simple rule on where the sequence ends (pre-registered test, 10 teams).
